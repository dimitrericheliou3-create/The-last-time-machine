## 1. Information processed
Depending on use, the service processes your email address and authentication identifiers; workspace name and membership; solution titles, problem descriptions, categories, steps, outcomes, and uploaded reference images; and basic technical information needed to operate and protect the service. Do not submit highly sensitive information the service is not designed to handle.

## 2. Purposes
Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide workspaces and knowledge-base features, support search and export, administer invitations, prevent abuse, maintain reliability, and respond to support requests.

## 3. Workspace visibility
Workspace content is intended to be available to authorized workspace members and service administrators when necessary for support or security. Workspace owners control invitations. Do not upload information unless you are permitted to share it with the workspace.

## 4. Providers and international processing
We use Supabase for authentication, database services, and private image storage. The Supabase project is configured in the US East (North Virginia) region. The application frontend is deployed separately from Supabase; the identity and processing location of that frontend host depend on the deployment configuration. Service providers may process information in other countries under their applicable terms and safeguards. We update this notice when our material providers or processing arrangements change.

## 5. Cookies and local storage
The current application does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Supabase authentication may use browser storage to keep a session signed in. If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking is added later, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before enabling it.

## 6. Retention and deletion
Information is retained while an account or workspace needs it to provide the service, subject to security, legal, backup, and dispute-resolution needs. Users can export solution records and initiate account deletion through Settings. Owned workspaces with other members must be transferred or have members removed before account deletion. Records in shared workspaces may be retained for other members; authored records may be reassigned to the workspace owner. Backups may persist temporarily until normal rotation.

## 7. Security
The service uses access controls and private storage policies intended to limit access to authorized workspaces. No internet service can promise absolute security. Users should keep credentials secure and avoid uploading secrets or highly sensitive personal data. Security controls must be tested before the service is represented as production-ready.
